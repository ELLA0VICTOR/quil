Your information in Quil.

Updated 30 October 2026

Quil is an experimental sales research app. It helps you review public business records and prepare introductions. This notice describes the implementation; the person operating a deployment controls its database and service accounts.

Accounts and saved research

A guest browser session can use limited live chat and research before sign-in. Quil stores its requests and usage against that session. Preview counters follow the session when it is adopted into a signed-in account.

Google Sign-In supplies your verified account identifier, email address and profile name. Quil stores them with a private session, your research requests, public source records, results, saved businesses, edited drafts and approved outreach records. Saved items may also be cached in your browser. Signing out clears that account’s browser caches; it does not delete its server records.

Gmail and Calendar are optional

Connecting Google grants separate permissions to send Gmail messages, read Gmail, create Calendar events and check availability. Google’s Gmail read permission is broad. Quil’s code restricts its reads to metadata in threads it created and searches for its generated message identifiers when reconciling an uncertain send. When you open a conversation, Quil also reads bounded message text from that selected thread. It does not scan unrelated inbox contents or load email attachments or remote images.

Quil requires a reviewed recipient and exact preview before sending a message or invitation. It checks reply metadata before follow-ups. Calendar suggestions use your availability; they do not establish the other person’s availability or acceptance.

Service providers

Live research sends search queries to Serper and the research brief plus selected evidence to OpenAI. Live chat sends your message, a bounded recent conversation window, the working brief and selected evidence from your own research to OpenAI to prepare an answer or clarify a new search. When you request a response to a business reply, bounded reply text is also sent to OpenAI to identify its intent and supporting quote. The reply assistant handles your private minimum price locally. Information you type directly into AI chat is included in that chat context. The OpenAI adapter requests no response storage. Google handles account authorization, approved mail and Calendar operations. A Vercel/Neon deployment stores application records in Neon. These providers also process data under their own policies.

If the operator enables Mozaik Cloud, Quil exports execution metadata such as agent roles, loop identifiers, timing and state changes. Quil’s exporter excludes research text, messages, recipients and credentials. It does not upload your Gmail or Calendar contents.

Storage and controls

Google tokens are encrypted on the server. Client secrets and provider API keys are not supplied to the browser. The operator must protect the encryption key and database credentials. Approved reply previews retain the message and offer terms you reviewed. This browser tab caches conversation messages, a bounded memory summary, working context and unsent input to preserve them through Google connection redirects. These browser caches are account-scoped and cleared on sign-out. Chat responses, retry records and selected reply analysis are cached within your account for one hour; reading a conversation does not save its full incoming bodies to the database. Research records are not automatically deleted after a fixed period. Disconnecting Google removes that stored connection and attempts to revoke its Google token; existing research and outreach records remain.

You can remove saved businesses in Quil, disconnect Google in Connections, and remove the app’s access in your Google Account permissions. For a request to remove other stored records or a privacy question, contact the operator identified on the Google consent screen. Do not post private data, connection strings or keys in public GitHub issues.

Public sources and accuracy

Public business records can be stale or incomplete. Missing website information does not establish that a website is absent. You review the evidence, recipient and wording before acting.
