# Quil demo walkthrough

Target length: about three minutes. Adjust to the submission form's recording limit.

## Finish the rehearsal

Use an approved live opportunity and a second inbox you control.

1. Send a reviewed test introduction from Quil to that inbox.
2. From the second inbox, reply: **Could you do it for USD 300?**
3. In Quil, open **Contact & next steps → Conversation → Read conversation**. Expand **Set your offer terms** and set scope **One booking page and handover**, quote **USD 500**, minimum **USD 400**, and delivery **10 working days**.
4. Choose **Prepare a response**. Check that the suggested fee stays at USD 400 and retains the scope and delivery terms. Review and approve the edited reply.
5. Reply from the second inbox with a future meeting timestamp including its timezone. Prepare the response, select **Review proposed meeting**, review availability and approve the invitation.
6. Accept from the second account, then choose **Contact → Activity → Check status** in Quil. Confirm **Invitation accepted**.

Business details now open separately from the chat. The top navigation switches between Evidence, Introduction, Offer and Contact; Contact contains Conversation, Write, Meeting and Activity. Use Close or Escape to return to your results. Switching sections or closing this view preserves your current edits during the same page session; refresh is a new session for unsent contact drafts.

Use a free time in your calendar and update the proposed timestamp for the day you rehearse. An explicit form such as `2026-11-02T14:00:00+01:00` carries the timezone through the handoff. Keep all rehearsal recipients under your control.
